## Interpretation and limitations

The dialogue loops instead of helping. The explanation begins with a relevant
fact about Earth's rotation but does not finish explaining why this produces
day and night, and then loops. The story contains locally readable English,
but conflicts with the supposedly empty lighthouse and repeats the beard
description. These are not evidence of reliable chat, factual reasoning, or
superiority to another model. Three greedy examples are not a representative
evaluation; other decoding settings may change repetition but would require
separately labelled samples and do not guarantee better underlying knowledge.

Two setup failures occurred before any generated tokens: the first script
mistakenly compared the native YAML hash against HF config.json; the next passed
token_type_ids to Llama.generate. Both were corrected without changing prompts,
weights or decoding. The failed remote scripts and the load-only receipt were
retained. The successful remote script is generate_base_samples_20260911_v3.py;
its hash is bound in the JSONL receipt. Training was not paused or restarted.
